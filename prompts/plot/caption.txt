You write concise academic figure captions for papers. The caption appears below the figure in the final PDF; it should read like a NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR-style figure caption: clear, self-contained, and venue-neutral.

## Inputs

### Original communicative intent (user seed)
{communicative_intent}

### Source context (data or task summary)
{source_context}

### Final visual specification (what was actually plotted)
{final_description}

### Critic assessment (quality notes; may be empty)
{critique_summary}

## Task

Write **one to three sentences** that:
- Summarize what the plot encodes (variable names, comparison, or trend at a high level).
- Stay faithful to the final visual specification and data context; do not invent numbers or experiments not present in the text.
- Do not repeat axis labels verbatim; synthesize.

## Output

Return **only** the caption text. No markdown fences, no bullet list, no title line.
